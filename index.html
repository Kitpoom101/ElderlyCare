How it works

Four steps, no legalese.

Most plans are finished in a week. You stay in control of every document and every decision.

1

Tell us what's going on

Answer a short questionnaire — on your own, or together with a parent. Thai or English, phone or computer.

2

Get a clear checklist

We flag the gaps that matter: missing will, no power of attorney, risky contracts, unclaimed benefits.

3

Meet your lawyer

We match you with a verified lawyer by issue, location and budget. Fixed fees, agreed upfront.

4

Keep it up to date

Documents live in a shared family vault. We remind you when something needs a second look.
